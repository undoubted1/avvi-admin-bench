# Brand assets

The dashboard follows the look of [avvi.cloud](https://avvi.cloud/).

- `avvi-logo.png`, `avvi-mark.png`: the Avvi wordmark and "A" mark from avvi.cloud (September 2026), trimmed and resized.
- `avvi-logo-dark.png`: the same wordmark with the navy "vv" turned white, for dark backgrounds.
- `og.png`: the link-preview card, laid out like the avvi.cloud share card.
- `../icon-*.png`, `../favicon.ico`: app and browser icons made from the site's 512px mark.
- `../fonts/`: Space Grotesk, Inter and JetBrains Mono (latin subsets), the site's fonts, served from here so the page makes no third-party requests. All three are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Brand colors (see the tokens at the top of `../app.css`): violet `#6D28D9`, gradient `#3300C8 → #6600C2`, ink `#15121C`, lines `#E9E8EE`, dark surface `#0E0A18`.
